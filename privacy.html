Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

OT Overtime Tracker is designed to help users record, calculate, and review their overtime information. Your privacy is important to us.

Information You Enter

OT Overtime Tracker allows you to enter information such as overtime hours, work schedules, hourly rates, travel time, overtime balances, and related notes. This information is used only to provide the app's overtime tracking and calculation features.

Data Storage

Overtime information entered into the app is stored locally on your device. OT Overtime Tracker does not operate its own server or database for collecting or storing your overtime records.

Subscriptions and Payments

OT Overtime Tracker offers an auto-renewable subscription. Purchases, subscription management, and payment processing are handled by Apple through the App Store. OT Overtime Tracker does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Data Collection

OT Overtime Tracker does not sell your personal information and does not use your information for advertising or tracking.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple's services where necessary to provide App Store purchases and subscription functionality. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

OT Overtime Tracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or OT Overtime Tracker, please use the contact information provided on the OT Overtime Tracker support page.
